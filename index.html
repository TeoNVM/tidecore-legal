Tidecore TD — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026
Developer: TeoNVM ("we", "us")
Contact: teomanolache96@gmail.com

This policy explains what happens to your information when you play Tidecore TD (the "Game") on Android, Windows or any other platform it is released on.

The short version

Tidecore TD does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The game itself works offline and everything it saves stays on your device. The only online feature is the optional in-app purchase, which is handled entirely by Google Play.

What the Game saves, and where

The Game saves the following only on your own device, so that you can continue playing:

  • your campaign progress (unlocked maps, stars, best levels);
  • your save games (map, level, scrap, turrets, hero upgrades);
  • your settings (volume, brightness, graphics quality, key bindings, sensitivity);
  • whether you have unlocked the full game (a yes/no flag, so the unlock also works offline).

On Android these files are in the Game's own app storage. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We have no access to them.

What the Game does not do

  • It has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, age or any other personal details.
  • It has no advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • It has no analytics, tracking, or crash-report upload.
  • It has no chat, multiplayer or other online features (only the optional purchase below).
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files outside its own storage.
  • It does not send any data over the internet, except that it asks Google Play whether the full game was bought (see below).

In-app purchase (Android)

Maps 1-3 are free. Maps 4-25 and the matching Island War islands are unlocked by a single optional purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We never see or store your payment details, name or email address. The Game only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the unlock, so it can restore it after a reinstall or on a new device, and stores the yes/no result on your device.

Permissions (Android)

The Game is built with Unreal Engine, which may list some standard Android permissions (for example network access and keeping the screen awake while you play). The Game uses the screen-awake permission so your phone does not lock in the middle of a level, and the Google Play billing permission (com.android.vending.BILLING) for the optional purchase. It does not use network access to send any information of its own.

App stores

If you download the Game from an app store (such as Google Play), the store may collect information about you (for example your account, purchase and device details) under its own privacy policy. We do not receive personal data from the store beyond anonymous, aggregated statistics the store shows to all developers (such as install counts).

Children

The Game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you live in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection laws (such as the GDPR), you keep all your rights under those laws, and you can contact us with any question at the address above.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game adds a feature that handles personal data (for example online play, cloud saves, ads or crash reports), we will update this policy before that version is released, and change the effective date above. The Google Play listing will always link to the current version.
